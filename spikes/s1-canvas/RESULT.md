server.arabic.latinSubset: the `arabic` subset files hold no ASCII (no Latin letters, digits, . , ( ) etc.) and two files registered under one alias do not merge (the first registered wins; the other's glyphs come from the system default), so with only `S1-Naskh` in ctx.font the Latin half of a mixed line and the full stop of an Arabic paragraph would come from the Mac's system fonts (boxes on a VPS without fonts, per the README). Each font's `latin` subset is therefore its own family and ctx.font is the stack `<arabic family>, <arabic family>-Latin`; a multi-family stack falls back glyph by glyph (measured). This deviates from "only the unique family in ctx.font" but uses only bundled families
